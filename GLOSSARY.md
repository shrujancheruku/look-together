# Look Together

An app for examining one's own understanding of life, inspired by the approach of Jiddu Krishnamurti: no lessons or set path, only inquiry into what a person brings.

## Language

**Problem**:
What a person brings to an Inquiry, in their own words (reworded only when they say yes to a rewording): something in their life they have to face and understand, not something the app promises to solve.
_Avoid_: Concern, issue, topic

**Inquiry**:
One session in which a person examines a Problem, from their first input through the Dialogue. Each Inquiry starts fresh and never draws on past Inquiries.
_Avoid_: Lesson, course, session, chat

**Assumption**:
A belief underneath what a person has said that they haven't examined. Assumptions are broken down, never judged true or false.
_Avoid_: Belief, error, misconception

**Breakdown**:
The structured set of Assumptions drawn out of a person's opening input at the start of an Inquiry, each shown to them as a Probe.
_Avoid_: Analysis, diagnosis, report

**Probe**:
The question in a Breakdown that pokes at one Assumption without stating it. The Dialogue on that Assumption starts from it.
_Avoid_: Prompt, opening question

**Dialogue**:
The back-and-forth that follows the Breakdown, in which the person explores their reaction to and understanding of the Assumptions, one Path at a time.
_Avoid_: Chat, conversation, coaching

**Path**:
The part of a Dialogue that follows one Probe, from the person choosing it to its end. A Path runs one way, without branches; the person can leave it and come back, and can read back over it.
_Avoid_: Thread, branch, tree

**Reflection**:
What a person gives the app at each step of a Path, written or dictated, then reviewed and edited before they choose to submit it. Until then it is not part of the Dialogue.
_Avoid_: Message, answer, reply, turn

**Open Question**:
The question a Path leaves standing, handed back to the person deliberately unanswered.
_Avoid_: Homework, takeaway, advice

**Angle**:
A change of approach the app makes when a Path is stuck: naming the stall as ours, narrowing to the one thread, slowing down, offering an analogy, or revealing the premise. An ordinary question is not an angle.
_Avoid_: Technique, tactic, hint, nudge

**Recap**:
What the person sees when an Inquiry ends: what was looked at, not what was learned. It lists the Assumptions explored, any change in understanding in the person's own words, the Open Questions, and the Assumptions not looked at yet.
_Avoid_: Summary, results, takeaways, report

**Shelf**:
All of a person's Inquiries on one device, and the rules that span them: at most 3 unfinished at once, each unfinished one ending by itself 7 days after it last moved on (opening or reading it doesn't count), and whether today's Inquiry can begin.
_Avoid_: Library, history, list, pond

**Step-out**:
The app leaving an Inquiry because the person may be at risk, to point them to people who can help. It names the risk gently: to the person, to others, or from others. An Inquiry that has been stepped out of never resumes.
_Avoid_: Escalation, intervention, crisis mode, trigger

**Soften**:
The app going gentler in an Inquiry when the person seems acutely distressed but not at risk, for the rest of that Inquiry. It never becomes a Step-out by repetition.
_Avoid_: De-escalate, calm mode

**Help**:
The control on every Inquiry screen that pauses the Inquiry and shows crisis contacts. It never ends the Inquiry.
_Avoid_: SOS, panic button, Step-out

**Report**:
A person telling Look Together that something the app wrote broke how it should reply, by reason. With their explicit choice, it can include that one exchange.
_Avoid_: Flag, complaint, feedback

**Account**:
Who a person is to Look Together: what they sign in with, so their daily allowance follows them across devices. It knows nothing of their Inquiries, which stay with the person, on their devices, never with us.
_Avoid_: Profile, user, member

**Region**:
Where a person's Account lives and their replies are made: the United States or the European Union, chosen once when they join and kept for as long as the Account exists.
_Avoid_: Server, location, data centre

**Pattern**:
An Assumption or friction that recurs across a person's Inquiries, shown to them only in a long-term view, outside any Inquiry.
_Avoid_: Insight, profile, progress

### The pixel world

**Shoreline**:
The one wide pixel-art world every screen is a view of, travelled left to right in the order of an Inquiry: the landing, home's pond, today's lily, the islets, and back ashore at the landing.
_Avoid_: Map, level, scene, background

**Landing**:
The bank at the left end of the Shoreline: the signpost (About), the boathouse (Settings), the welcome, and the spot where a Recap opens after the journey.
_Avoid_: Menu, lobby

**Islet**:
One of the small themed islands a Path crosses, one per step, each joined to the last by a bridge. Which islets a Path crosses comes from the Inquiry's seed, never from anything the person did.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, checkpoint

**Walker**:
The small figure that stands for the person on the Shoreline, set up by them on the Make yourself at home step or in Settings. It walks one islet per Reflection and rows the boat; it never speaks, gestures or leads.
_Avoid_: Avatar, character, guide, companion

**Lily**:
How an Inquiry appears in the world: a cluster of pads that grows as the Inquiry does, ages while it is unfinished, and flowers when it reaches its Recap.
_Avoid_: Plant, tree, progress

**Pad**:
One leaf of a lily: the first stands for the Problem, and one more unfurls for each Probe. Pads never change when a Path ends, so a lily never shows progress.
_Avoid_: Leaf, node, tile

**Frost panel**:
The pane of frosted pixel art that holds a group of words over the world, tinted sky above the waterline and water below it.
_Avoid_: Card, glass, halo, overlay

**Reading mode**:
How the world behaves while a person reads or writes: it runs at half speed and the frost panels thicken, so the words come first.
_Avoid_: Focus mode, pause

**Cue**:
A short one-off animation the world plays when an Inquiry's state changes or the person does something in the world, from a closed list. A cue never replays, never hides words or holds back a reply, and never rewards or counts anything. The one wait it causes: the next question appears only once the walker sits, at most 2 s after Submit.
_Avoid_: Reward, effect, celebration, feedback

**Still water**:
The setting, and the matching colours, in which the world stops moving altogether and goes quiet. Help uses the same still colours.
_Avoid_: Pause, low-power mode

**Soundscape**:
The looping sound of the place the camera is on: water, wind and birds, never a melody. It goes quiet wherever the world stops moving, and whenever the person dictates or listens to a reply.
_Avoid_: Music, soundtrack, ambience track

**Cue sound**:
The short sound a cue makes, always the noise of the thing itself (footsteps, oars, water). Not every cue has one, and nothing the person taps makes one.
_Avoid_: SFX, sound effect, chime, audio feedback

**Make yourself at home**:
The step after the welcome where the person may choose their season, whether the world has sound, and their walker. Nothing on it has to be chosen.
_Avoid_: Personalise, onboarding, preferences, customise

**Leaf note**:
A note from the guide on the person's first Inquiry, set beside the thing it explains while everything else dims.
_Avoid_: Tooltip, tutorial, coach mark
