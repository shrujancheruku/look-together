# Look Together

An app for examining one's own understanding of life, inspired by the approach of Jiddu Krishnamurti: no lessons or set path, only inquiry into what a person brings.

## Language

**Problem**:
What a person brings to an Inquiry, in their own words (reworded only when they say yes to a rewording): something in their life they have to face and understand, not something the app promises to solve.
_Avoid_: Concern, issue, topic

**Inquiry**:
One session in which a person examines a Problem, from their first input through the Dialogue. Each Inquiry starts fresh and never draws on past Inquiries.
_Avoid_: Lesson, course, session, chat

**Assumption**:
A belief underneath what a person has said that they haven't examined. Assumptions are broken down, never judged true or false.
_Avoid_: Belief, error, misconception

**Breakdown**:
The structured set of Assumptions drawn out of a person's opening input at the start of an Inquiry, each shown to them as a Probe.
_Avoid_: Analysis, diagnosis, report

**Probe**:
The question in a Breakdown that pokes at one Assumption without stating it. The Dialogue on that Assumption starts from it.
_Avoid_: Prompt, opening question

**Dialogue**:
The back-and-forth that follows the Breakdown, in which the person explores their reaction to and understanding of the Assumptions, one Path at a time.
_Avoid_: Chat, conversation, coaching

**Path**:
The part of a Dialogue that follows one Probe, from the person choosing it to its end. A Path runs one way, without branches; the person can leave it and come back, and can read back over it.
_Avoid_: Thread, branch, tree

**Reflection**:
What a person gives the app at each step of a Path, written or dictated, then reviewed and edited before they choose to submit it. Until then it is not part of the Dialogue.
_Avoid_: Message, answer, reply, turn

**Open Question**:
A question handed back to the person, deliberately unanswered, when a Dialogue can find no further angle of questioning.
_Avoid_: Homework, takeaway, advice

**Recap**:
What the person sees when an Inquiry ends: what was looked at, not what was learned. It lists the Assumptions explored, any change in understanding in the person's own words, the Open Questions, and the Assumptions not looked at yet.
_Avoid_: Summary, results, takeaways, report

**Step-out**:
The app leaving an Inquiry because the person may be at risk, to point them to people who can help. An Inquiry that has been stepped out of never resumes.
_Avoid_: Escalation, intervention, crisis mode

**Report**:
A person telling Look Together that something the app wrote broke how it should reply, by reason. With their explicit choice, it can include that one exchange.
_Avoid_: Flag, complaint, feedback

**Account**:
Who a person is to Look Together: what they sign in with, so their daily allowance follows them across devices. It knows nothing of their Inquiries, which stay with the person, on their devices, never with us.
_Avoid_: Profile, user, member

**Pattern**:
An Assumption or friction that recurs across a person's Inquiries, shown to them only in a long-term view, outside any Inquiry.
_Avoid_: Insight, profile, progress
