# What the Krishnamurti Foundations permit for an app inspired by his teachings

Research for [#3](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/look-together/issues/3). Researched 2026-10-29 against the Foundations' own sites.

> **Not legal advice.** This summarises what the Foundations publish. It is not a legal opinion on copyright, trademark or fair use in any jurisdiction. Before launch, have a lawyer review the store listing and any use of his name, words or image.

## Short answer

- **Copyright in his work belongs to two Foundations.** The Krishnamurti Foundation of America (KFA) and the Krishnamurti Foundation Trust (KFT, UK) say they are the *sole* copyright holders of Krishnamurti's work. Krishnamurti Foundation India (KFI) holds copyright in its own site content. [S1][S2][S6]
- **Quoting needs permission, except perhaps a small extract.** The Foundations say a small extract *may* fall under fair use. Anything else goes through their Permission Request Form. They grant or refuse at their sole discretion and take about 2–3 weeks to reply. [S2][S3]
- **AI is their stated concern.** KFT's Authenticity statement (updated September 2026) warns about AI that produces "Krishnamurti" text or holds dialogue emulating him. It repeats that he wanted no interpreters of his teachings, and says the Foundations are the only guaranteed source of the authentic teachings. [S4]
- **There is no published policy on using his name, and no endorsement programme.** None of the three sites has a trademark, brand-use or endorsement page. Saying the app is "inspired by" him, in plain words, is not covered by anything the Foundations publish. The risk is implying that they endorse or are affiliated with the app.
- **The route to them** is the permission form for content, and their published contacts for anything else (see [Contacts](#contacts)).

## What look-together can and cannot do

These follow from the sources below. They are recommendations, not anything the Foundations have said about this app.

| Use | Position | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saying "inspired by J. Krishnamurti" in onboarding, the about page and the store listing | Low risk, as long as it is plainly worded and does not suggest endorsement | No name-use policy found; S1 shows copyright, not the name, is what they assert |
| The founder's story as an alumnus of a Foundation school | Fine as a statement of fact. Do not suggest the school or Foundation backs the app | No policy found |
| A clear line such as "look-together is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Krishnamurti Foundations" | Recommended | Answers the authenticity concern in S4 |
| Linking out to the Foundations' sites for his actual words (the later Readings effort) | Recommended. This is the use they invite | S4 names the Foundations as the authentic source; S5 says KFT puts his work out free |
| Quoting him in the app or store listing | Avoid in the MVP (the map already rules it out). If needed later, file the permission form. Even short quotes are only *possibly* fair use | S2, S3 |
| Using his photograph | Avoid without permission. The author photos are cleared for book publication, need a copyright credit, and require contacting K Publications first | S7 |
| The AI speaking as Krishnamurti, imitating his voice or style, or putting words in his mouth | **Do not.** This is exactly what S4 warns about | S4 |
| Presenting the Dialogue as "Krishnamurti's method" or as an interpretation of his teaching | Avoid. Frame it as the app's own way of inquiring, inspired by him | S4 ("no interpreters") |
| Using Foundation names or logos, or a name or icon that could be mistaken for theirs | Avoid. KFA has registered its name as a US trademark (secondary source, unverified) | S8 |
| Including his texts in model prompts, fine-tuning or retrieval | Treat it as using copyrighted material: it needs permission. It also risks the distorted "Krishnamurti" output that S4 describes | S1, S3, S4 |

## Findings

### 1. Who owns what

- K Publications is the joint publishing body of the Foundations in America, England, Spain and India. It states that KFA and KFT Ltd are the sole copyright holders of the work of J. Krishnamurti. [S1]
- The permission form says the same: copyright for all his works is owned by KFA and/or KFT, and the Foundations are responsible for preserving and spreading his teachings. [S3]
- Since 2007, KFA and KFT have published together as "K Publications" and contract outside publishers such as HarperCollins and Shambhala. [S5]
- Copyright notices on the sites: KFA's footer reads "All Content Copyright © Krishnamurti Foundation of America" [S5]. KFT's reads "© Krishnamurti Foundation Trust Ltd" [S6]. KFI's says "Copyright © 2025, Krishnamurti Foundation India. All Rights Reserved" [S9].
- KFT is a UK charity (company 01055588, charity 312865) set up in 1968 to preserve his teachings and make them available, largely for free. [S6]

### 2. Quoting and excerpts

- The K Publications permission page says a small extract of his published material *may* fall under fair use, and links to the US Copyright Office's fair-use factsheet. If unsure, file the form. [S2]
- The form covers written, audio and video works. It asks for the project type, the format (it lists "Website" and "Audio/Video digital download" but has no "app" option, so use "Other"), whether the project is commercial, how large a share of the project the material is, a sample, any further rights needed (worldwide distribution, translation), and the exact source passages. [S3]
- The form says the decision to grant or refuse rests solely with the copyright holder (KFA or KFT), and to allow 2–3 weeks for a reply from the "Krishnamurti copyrights department". [S3]
- Fair use is a US doctrine. Other countries have their own, often narrower, quotation exceptions, and an app distributed worldwide may fall under several of them. This is a reason to avoid quotes rather than rely on fair use.

### 3. AI and authenticity (the most relevant source for this app)

KFT's page "The Krishnamurti Foundations – Authenticity" (published 2024-05-16, last modified 2026-09-03) [S4]:

- It explains the long history of careful transcription and editing that he began himself, so that his words are not misrepresented.
- It warns that anyone can now attribute words to him online, and that AI can produce a "Krishnamurti" text or hold a dialogue emulating him within seconds. It sees a danger of AI becoming a distorted authority on what he said and meant.
- It says he made clear there are to be no interpreters of his teachings, and applies this equally to AI bots and future video avatars.
- It concludes that the Foundations (and their chosen publishers) are the only guaranteed resource for the authentic teachings, framed "not a statement of authority" but as a matter of care.

This is not a licence term, but it is the clearest published statement of where the Foundations would see a conflict. For look-together, staying clear of conflict means: the AI never speaks as him or for him, never quotes or paraphrases him as authority, and the app never presents itself as his teaching.

### 4. Images

- K Publications provides high-resolution author portraits, described as royalty-free but needing a copyright credit, for use in published books. Users must contact them first to get the correct credit. Rights holders vary by photo: KFT, KFA, the Estate of Mary Zimbalist, or "Courtesy Krishnamurti Foundations". [S7]
- Nothing on the site covers using his image in software, an app icon or marketing. Assume permission is needed.

### 5. Name, trademark and endorsement

- None of kfoundation.org, kfa.org or kpublications.com has a trademark, brand-use, name-use or endorsement page. Their sitemaps were checked for such pages and none were found. [S5][S6]
- Third-party records of USPTO data show "KRISHNAMURTI FOUNDATION OF AMERICA" registrations, with "FOUNDATION OF AMERICA" disclaimed in one. They show no mark on "Krishnamurti" alone. [S8] *This is a secondary source and is unverified.* The USPTO's TSDR API now requires a key and could not be queried here. A proper search of the USPTO, the UK IPO and WIPO is recommended before launch.
- No endorsement or approval programme is published. Any review or blessing would be an informal request to the contacts below.

### 6. Contacts

- **Copyright permission (KFA/KFT):** the Permission Request Form on kpublications.com. [S2][S3]
- **K Publications, publications enquiries:** publications@kfoundation.org covers USA, Brazil, Asia, Australasia, India, Europe, Africa and the Middle East. publicaciones@fkla.org covers Spain and Latin America. [S1]
- **KFT general:** info@kfoundation.org. [S6]
- **KFA general:** contact form at kfa.org/contact-us. [S5]

## Gaps and caveats

- **jkrishnamurti.org** (the Foundations' joint official site) returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches, and its archived terms page was rate-limited. Its terms of use were **not read**. Read them by hand before relying on this document.
- **KFI's** own policies on reusing its content were not found. KFI runs the Foundation schools in India, but nothing it publishes addresses alumni talking about their school.
- The trademark position comes from secondary sources only (see 5).
- Nothing here is a statement by the Foundations about look-together itself. Only they can give that.
