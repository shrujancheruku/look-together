# An Account's region is chosen once, by latency, and found by asking every region at sign-in

Every Account lives in one region, `us` or `eu`. Its record, meters, counters, logs and crash reports stay there, and its replies are made there. The region is fixed for as long as the Account exists. At sign-in the device asks **both** regions whether they hold the identity: an Apple or Google token is verified at each, and for an emailed code only the region holding the Account sends it. If one does, that is the person's region, with nothing to choose. If neither does, the person chooses a region before the Account is created. The default is whichever region answers the device faster: 1 warm-up and 3 timed pings to each, compared by median, with no tiebreak. Web does the same, and it keeps the region only in its session. We chose this over a fixed country table from IP geolocation because GDPR doesn't require EU storage and latency already sends Europeans to `eu`, while letting each person see and pick their region for themselves. We chose it over choosing the region before sign-in because someone on a second device could pick the other region and silently get a second Account, with a second daily allowance. We chose it over a settings switch because moving an Account, its meters and its counts between regions is work and risk the MVP doesn't need. The cost: the identity briefly reaches the region that doesn't hold it, for the lookup only, never stored and never logged. And changing region means exporting, deleting the Account and joining again in the other region.
