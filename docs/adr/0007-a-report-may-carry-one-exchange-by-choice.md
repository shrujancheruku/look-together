# A Report may carry one exchange, by the person's choice

A person can Report a Probe or a Path reply that judged, led, preached, sounded like therapy or was unsafe, and may choose, each time, to send that one exchange with it: a deliberate, narrow exception to [ADR 0003](0003-inquiry-content-never-on-our-servers.md). The stores only need a content-free report, so this is for learning, not compliance; we chose it over reasons-only because counts alone say that a hard line broke but never how, and after the closed beta there is no other way to see it. To keep the exception narrow, the exchange is only the reported text and what it answered, names and contact details are masked before the person sees the preview (which they can send or not, never edit), sending is an unticked toggle with a disclaimer that serves as explicit consent, and it is never offered in a stepped-out Inquiry. What is sent stays in the person's region for 30 days, apart from Accounts and with no Account or Inquiry id, is read only by the founder, never reaches a model, a third party or the evaluation set, and can be deleted from the device at any time.
