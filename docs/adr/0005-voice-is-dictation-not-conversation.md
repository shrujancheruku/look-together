# Voice is dictation into a Reflection, not a spoken conversation

Speaking to the app only fills in a Reflection: the person dictates, reads back, edits and submits when they are done thinking, and the app's replies come as text, read aloud only when the person taps "listen". We chose this over a spoken Dialogue with turn-taking (a Done tap, or silence and end-of-turn detection, with barge-in), because the words being out of someone's mouth doesn't mean their thinking is over: an Inquiry needs room to review a reflection before it counts, and a conversation, however well it detects pauses, pushes the person to answer at its pace. It also drops the hardest machinery for the MVP: self-hosted LiveKit, the voice worker and its VM in each region give way to streaming Cloud STT and a TTS call on the backend. The cost is that the app no longer feels like talking with someone, and that no part of a draft, including the dictation still in progress, is ever seen by the distress classifier until it is submitted.
