# Sign-in is required, and an Account knows nothing of Inquiries

The MVP requires a person to sign in (Apple, Google or an emailed code) before their first Inquiry, reversing the "no accounts" part of [ADR 0003](0003-inquiry-content-never-on-our-servers.md). We chose this over keying the daily limit to an install or browser because any paid version needs an Account that carries across iOS, Android and web, so testing without one would test the wrong product, and because the business needs a way to reach people who opt in. Its cost is the privacy headline: we now know who a person is, so the promise becomes "we know who you are, never what you said". To keep that promise, the Account is a closed record (id, email or Apple relay, provider, created date, region, platform, marketing consent) kept in the person's region, the usage meters are keyed to it, and nothing about any Inquiry is ever joined to it: no Problems, no per-person counts of Inquiries, Recaps or Step-outs; product analytics are aggregate only. Because Firebase Authentication runs only in the US, we verify Apple and Google sign-ins on our own backend and keep Accounts in regional Firestore, at the cost of building and maintaining that auth ourselves. Apple may reject required sign-in under guideline 5.1.1(v); the fallback is one Inquiry before sign-in.
