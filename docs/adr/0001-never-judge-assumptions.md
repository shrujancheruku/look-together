# The app never judges an Assumption true or false

Following Krishnamurti's rejection of authority, the app only questions: it draws out Assumptions and asks until the person sees for themselves, and never declares an Assumption right, wrong, true or false. When a Dialogue is stuck, it tries another angle of questioning, and failing that leaves an Open Question rather than a verdict. We chose this over stating conclusions (or stating them only when someone is stuck) because making the app the authority would contradict the approach it exists to share.

_Amended by [Are outcomes and the Recap a verdict in disguise?](https://github.com/shrujancheruku/look-together/issues/205): the app also never judges whether the person challenged an Assumption. It records only what the person did with it (rejected it, looked at it, set it down, or didn't look at it)._
