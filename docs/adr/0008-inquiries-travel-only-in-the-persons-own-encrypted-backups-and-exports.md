# Inquiries travel only in the person's own encrypted backups and exports

Inquiries now go into the person's own iCloud or Android backup and come back on a restore, even to a new phone, reversing [ADR 0003](0003-inquiry-content-never-on-our-servers.md)'s "a lost device loses its Inquiries". We chose this over excluding them from backups because people already trust their phone's backup with far more important data, and losing every Inquiry on a new phone is a cost they'd feel without ever having chosen it. To keep the content unreadable to Apple and Google as well as to us, each Account's database key travels separately from the database: in iCloud Keychain on iOS, which is end-to-end encrypted even when iCloud Backup is not, and in Block Store on Android. On Android, cloud backup is allowed only when it is end-to-end encrypted (Android 9+ with a screen lock), and device-to-device transfer is always allowed. The cost is native code that Expo doesn't provide (`react-native-keychain` with `cloudSync`, a Block Store module and our own backup rules), a privacy claim that rests on Apple's and Google's encryption working as they document, and a restored database whose key didn't come back, which the person must be shown rather than have fail silently. Backups never cross platforms, so moving between iOS, Android and web goes through a JSON export the person makes and imports themselves. Our own end-to-end-encrypted cloud restore stays post-MVP.
